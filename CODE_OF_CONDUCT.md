# Community code of conduct

We follow the principles of the Contributor Covenant: provide a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

Use considerate language, accept constructive feedback, respect privacy and focus on the project. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, sexualized behavior and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and fair, allowing correction where appropriate. These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through an established private channel. If none exists, ask for a private contact without publishing sensitive details. Maintainers must protect reporters’ privacy and avoid retaliation. A maintainer with a conflict should recuse themselves.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
