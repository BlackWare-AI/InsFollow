
InsFollow 0.1 is the initial user-assisted Android follow-workflow manager.

Favorites import and folder selection feed a persistent deduplicated shuffled queue. Users act through Instagram's genuine WebView interface; results are refreshed and checked before being saved. Includes local history, scheduling, interruption recovery, English/Turkish AMOLED UI, JSON/TXT reports, annotated Favorites copies and app backup/restore.

Requires Android 14+. Independent and unofficial; no affiliation with Instagram or Meta. No automatic Follow execution, challenge bypass or account safety guarantee. Collapsing the browser or leaving the Activity pauses processing. See the tagged README and docs/VALIDATION.md for precise validation evidence and limitations.
